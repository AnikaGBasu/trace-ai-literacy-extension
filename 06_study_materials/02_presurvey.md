# Presurvey

Reference instrument • materials-1.0.0.

Study ID: assigned by the researcher. Do not enter your name or school. You may select “Prefer not to answer” for any survey item. Answer about the **past 30 days** unless the question says otherwise.

| ID | Question | Response options |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PRE01 | How often have you used a generative AI chatbot for any purpose? | Never; Less than once a week; 1–2 days a week; 3–5 days a week; Every day or almost every day; Prefer not to answer |
| PRE02 | How often have you used a generative AI chatbot for school-related work or studying? | Same options as PRE01 |
| PRE03 | When using generative AI for school-related work, how often did you copy some of its generated text into your notes or work? | Never; Rarely; Sometimes; Often; Almost always; I did not use AI for school-related work; Prefer not to answer |
| PRE04 | When an AI response included a factual claim that mattered for your work, how often did you check it using a source outside the chatbot? | Never; Rarely; Sometimes; Often; Almost always; I did not encounter this situation; Prefer not to answer |
| PRE05 | How would you rate your ability to use a generative AI chatbot for a task you want to complete? | 1 Very low; 2 Low; 3 Moderate; 4 High; 5 Very high; Prefer not to answer |
| PRE06 | Before today, how aware were you that a generative AI chatbot can give a confident answer containing incorrect information? | 1 Not at all aware; 2 Slightly aware; 3 Moderately aware; 4 Very aware; 5 Extremely aware; Prefer not to answer |

## Optional topic-familiarity items

Ask these items without teaching the topics or naming any assessment claim.

For each topic below, choose: **0 I have not heard of it; 1 I recognize the term; 2 I know a little; 3 I could give a basic explanation; 4 I could explain and apply it; Prefer not to answer**.

- PRE07A: How neurons send signals using action potentials.
- PRE07B: How collaborative filtering produces recommendations.
- PRE07C: How confirmation bias can influence judgment.


## Coding

Keep “not applicable,” “prefer not to answer,” and skipped responses distinct from the lowest rating. Store them as missing values with a separate missing-reason field. Do not recode “never” as missing. These presurvey scales do not define the intervention-response engagement scale, which is exclusively **0, 1, or 2**.
