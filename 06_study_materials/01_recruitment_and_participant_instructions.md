# Recruitment and participant instructions

Reference instrument • materials-1.0.0.

## Recruitment notice

**Help us study how students use AI while learning**

We invite high-school students ages 14–18 in grades 9–12 to participate in a study of AI-assisted learning. Participants will use an AI chat interface to learn three short topics, prepare notes, answer questions without their notes, and give feedback about the interface. Some versions may display brief reflection prompts before copied text is used.

You do not need to be an expert in the topics. Participation is voluntary. You may decline, skip optional questions or reflection prompts, or stop. Your choice and your performance will not affect your school grades or access to educational activities.

The study uses a practice activity and three seven-minute learning periods, with additional time for consent, questionnaires, reflections, and assessments. Session arrangements and the study contact are provided with the invitation.

## Participant instructions

You will complete three learning rounds using different versions of an AI chat interface. In each round, your goal is to understand one concept well enough to explain it and apply it later.

During learning:

- Read the task goal before you begin.
- Use only the study's ChatGPT interface and notes workspace.
- Ask follow-up questions whenever useful. You may copy, paraphrase, or write your own notes.
- Do not enter your name, school, contact information, personal account details, or private information about yourself or others into the chat or forms.
- If a reflection appears, respond in your own words or select **Skip for Now**. A skip reason is optional. The learning clock pauses during the reflection.
- Tell the researcher if a button, timer, or page does not work.

After learning, ChatGPT and your notes will be hidden. Answer the explanation and application questions from memory. You will also evaluate several claims and rate your confidence and annoyance. These ratings ask about your experience; there is no preferred rating.

Some assessment statements are deliberately questionable. You are not expected to accept a claim because it is printed in a study form or sounds fluent. You will receive corrections at the end of the session.

Please keep each round's content separate. Do not return to previous notes or use another AI tool, web search, messaging, or outside reference material. You may ask the researcher for interface help. The researcher will not provide topic answers during a task.
