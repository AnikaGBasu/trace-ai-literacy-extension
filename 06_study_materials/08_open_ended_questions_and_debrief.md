# Open-ended questions and debrief

Reference instrument • materials-1.0.0.

## Core open-ended prompts

**changed_behavior_reflection:** Did any version change how you read, used, checked, or explained the AI output? Describe a specific example if you can. “No change” is an acceptable answer.

**negative_feedback_reflection:** What, if anything, made the reflection versions inconvenient, confusing, unnecessary, or frustrating? Describe the moment and what you would change. “No concerns” is an acceptable answer.

## Optional elaboration prompts

1. What led you to choose your preferred version? Consider what you gained and what it required of you.
2. Describe a prompt that fit your task particularly well or poorly. What made it fit or not fit?
3. Were there moments when you skipped, wanted to skip, or wanted more support? What was happening at that moment?
4. Did you feel free to decide whether to copy, rewrite, verify, or continue? Explain.
5. For which kinds of schoolwork would you want this tool enabled or disabled?
6. What one change would most improve the tool for you?

Use the same selected elaboration prompts for everyone, with neutral follow-ups.

## Researcher debrief script

“Thank you for participating. We are studying how different AI interfaces affect learning and the experience of using AI. The reflection prompts were designed to encourage students to explain, apply, or check information at the moment of copying. We are also interested in when these prompts feel unnecessary or disruptive. Your feedback can include both positive and negative experiences.

“Some claims in the assessments were deliberately questionable. In task A, a stronger stimulus does not simply make each individual action potential proportionally taller. In task B, a user with no interaction history creates a cold-start challenge; access to other users' histories does not automatically reveal that new user's preferences. In task C, conflicting evidence does not automatically remove confirmation bias. People can dismiss or reinterpret evidence that challenges a belief.

“The assessment examples do not say anything about your honesty or your value as a learner. AI outputs and study statements should both be examined when accuracy matters. You may ask about the activity or use the study's contact route if you have a question later.”

Use the participant's approved study contact route for questions or withdrawal requests; do not add contact identifiers to this anonymous package. Provide the topic correction sheet only after all experimental assessments are complete.
