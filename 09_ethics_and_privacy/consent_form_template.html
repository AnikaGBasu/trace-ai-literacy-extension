AI interfaces and student learning

Please read this information before deciding whether to take part.

What will I do?

You will complete a survey and practice activity, then use three AI interfaces to learn three topics. Each learning period provides seven minutes of active chat time. Some versions may show a brief reflection when you copy text. Afterwards, the chat and your notes will be hidden while you answer questions. At the end, you will give feedback.

What might feel uncomfortable?

You may find questions difficult or reflection prompts slow or annoying. Some assessment claims are deliberately questionable and will be explained at the end. You do not need to describe private information.

Do I have to take part?

No. You may decline, stop, skip optional survey items, or bypass an intervention. Your decision will not affect school grades or access to educational activities.

What information is used?

The study records task answers, ratings, and interaction information under a study ID. The interface may send the task prompt, AI excerpt, selected excerpt, and reflection to a model service. Do not enter names, school names, contact details, or private information. The researcher will explain the study’s access, retention, withdrawal, sharing, and applicable consent/assent arrangements.

Who can I ask?

Ask the researcher or use the study contact given in your invitation. Identifying contacts are omitted from this anonymous template.

Your choice

By selecting the affirmative button, you indicate that you have read the information, have had an opportunity to ask questions, and voluntarily agree under the procedure explained to you. Neither choice is preselected.

No name, email, or school is collected by this template. Consent records are kept separately from outcome data.
