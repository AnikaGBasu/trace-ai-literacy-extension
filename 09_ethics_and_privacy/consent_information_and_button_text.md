# Participant information and affirmative consent/assent interaction

Reference participant-information form • consent-form-1.0.0.

## Participant-facing information

**Study: AI interfaces and student learning**

You are invited to take part in a study of how students use an AI chat interface while learning. Please read this page before deciding.

**What will I do?** You will complete a short survey and practice activity, then use three versions of an AI interface to learn three topics. Each learning period provides seven minutes of active chat time. Some versions may show a brief reflection when you copy text. Afterwards, the chat and your notes will be hidden while you answer questions. At the end, you will give feedback about the versions.

**What might feel uncomfortable?** You may find some questions difficult, be uncertain about an answer, or find reflection prompts slow or annoying. Some assessment claims are deliberately questionable and will be explained at the end. You do not need to describe personal experiences or private information.

**Do I have to take part?** No. You may decline, stop, skip optional survey items, or bypass an intervention. Your decision will not affect school grades or access to educational activities. Completing a reflection is not a condition of continuing voluntary participation.

**What information is used?** The study records your task answers, ratings, and interaction information such as copying and reflection timing under a study ID. The interface may send the task prompt, AI excerpt, selected excerpt, and your reflection to the configured model service to create or evaluate a reflection. Do not enter names, school names, contact details, or private information about yourself or others. The researcher will explain the study's actual access, retention, withdrawal, and sharing arrangements through the accompanying approved information before collecting data.

**Who can I ask?** Use the researcher contact provided with your invitation or ask the researcher now. Identifying contact details are intentionally omitted from this anonymous supplement.

**Your choice:** By selecting the affirmative button, you indicate that you have read the information, have had an opportunity to ask questions, and voluntarily agree to participate under the consent/assent procedure explained to you. A declined choice ends the form without starting the study. Neither choice is preselected.

Affirmative button: **“I agree to participate”**

Decline button: **“I do not agree to participate”**

## Form behavior

The HTML template presents two explicit, unselected buttons. After a click, it displays the decision and enables download of a local form record containing the template version, choice, and click time. The template makes no network request and does not enroll a participant.
